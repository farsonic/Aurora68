# Aurora68 Display's Balloon Help (PiDisplay's until 21 November 2026) (System 7), one block per menu, in the menus' order (tools/mac/mkhmnu.py)
menu 128
title = The Apple menu: About Aurora68 Display, and the desk accessories.
item Tells which version of Aurora68 Display this is and what it is for. | Tells about Aurora68 Display. Not available while a dialog is open.
item -
menu 129
title = File menu: close Aurora68 Display's window, save the report, quit.
item Closes the window, which ends Aurora68 Display. | Closes the window. Not available now.
item Saves the report about the monitor and its resolutions as a TeachText document. | Saves the report. Not available while a desk accessory is in front.
item -
item Quits Aurora68 Display. Nothing is changed unless Restart to Apply was used. | Quits Aurora68 Display.
menu 130
title = Edit menu: Copy puts the report about the monitor on the Clipboard.
item = Undo is not available in Aurora68 Display: nothing in its window is edited.
item -
item = Cut is not available in Aurora68 Display: nothing in its window is edited.
item Copies the report about the monitor and its resolutions to the Clipboard, as text. | Copies the report.
item = Paste is not available in Aurora68 Display: nothing in its window is edited.
item = Clear is not available in Aurora68 Display: nothing in its window is edited.
item -
item = Select All is not available in Aurora68 Display: Copy always takes the whole report.
menu 131
title Display menu: choose the suggested resolution, go back to the settings in force, or apply your choice. | Display menu. Not available until the Pi's kernel has answered.
item Selects the resolution Aurora68 Display suggests for this monitor (the one with the bullet). | Selects the suggested resolution. Not available: it is already chosen, or there is none.
item Puts the resolution and rotation back to the ones in force. | Puts back the settings in force. Not available: nothing has been changed.
item -
item Writes your choice to the SD card and restarts; a new resolution restarts the Pi as well. | Applies your choice. Not available: nothing has been changed, or the SD card cannot be written.
